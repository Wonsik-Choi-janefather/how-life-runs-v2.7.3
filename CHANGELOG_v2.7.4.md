# How Life Runs v2.7.4

Wonsik Choi and Jeongin Choi

Released 22 October 2026.

This revision adds five connected audit criteria: executable inheritance; resource boundaries including external supply; information and interpretation machinery; joint lineage and aggregate checks; and observation versus control. Chapters 10, 13, 14, 15 and 24 are expanded. Chapter 26 adds cross-study records and evaluation criteria. Chapter 27 adds priorities and distinguishes proposals from completed results.

The English and Korean GitBook chapters contain the additions. The PDF and Word files in downloads/v2.7.4 are the updated Korean manuscript. This release does not relabel the archived v2.7.3 English PDF as a v2.7.4 translation. The existing archive folder remains a historical source snapshot.
